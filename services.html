Hormone Consultation

60 minutes

A comprehensive hormone consultation designed to identify imbalances and create a personalized, precision-driven treatment plan based on your symptoms, goals, and lab work. This service includes an InBody scan and a detailed discussion of testosterone therapy, estradiol therapy for women, progesterone support, and thyroid optimization. Hormone delivery options may include topical creams, injectables, and pellet therapy—tailored to best support your individual needs.

Hormone Pelleting Procedure

45 minutes

During this in-house procedure, small bioidentical hormone pellets—typically testosterone and/or estradiol—are placed just beneath the skin using local anesthetic for comfort. The pellets provide a steady, consistent release of hormones over time, helping to support energy, mood, mental clarity, metabolism, libido, and overall vitality without the fluctuations of other methods. Each treatment is fully customized to your symptoms, goals, and lab work, offering a minimally invasive, low-downtime solution for long-term hormone balance.

Therapeutic Phlebotomy

45 minutes

An in-house service for patients undergoing hormone therapy, provided when clinically indicated as part of their treatment plan. This service supports safe, effective hormone optimization while offering the convenience of receiving care without the need to visit an external donation center.

15 minutes

Peptide Therapy

30 minutes

Peptide therapy is an advanced, targeted approach to optimizing health at a cellular level. Peptides are naturally occurring amino acid chains that act as signaling molecules in the body, helping regulate key functions such as metabolism, fat loss, muscle growth, energy, and recovery. Peptides can support weight loss, improve body composition, enhance recovery, boost energy, and promote overall wellness and longevity. Each treatment plan is fully customized to your goals, utilizing specific peptides to support metabolic health, performance, and healthy aging.

Medical Aesthetics

Subtle, natural-looking enhancements administered by skilled medical professionals.

Aesthetic Consultation

45 minutes

A personalized aesthetic consultation designed to assess your concerns, skin health, and overall goals with a refined, expert approach. During your visit, we take the time to understand your vision and create a customized, precision-driven treatment plan that enhances your natural features while maintaining balance and harmony. Recommendations may include neurotoxins, dermal fillers, biostimulators, advanced skin rejuvenation procedures, and medical-grade skincare—thoughtfully selected to deliver subtle, natural-looking results aligned with your goals.

Neurotoxins

30 minutes

Neurotoxin treatments are designed to soften the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles while maintaining natural facial movement. Common treatment areas may include the forehead, frown lines, and crow’s feet for a smoother, refreshed appearance.

Dermal Fillers

60 minutes

Dermal fillers are designed to restore lost volume, enhance facial contours, and create balanced, natural-looking results. Treatment areas may include temples, cheeks, lips, chin, and jawline based on your individual aesthetic goals.

Biostimulators

45 minutes

Biostimulators, such as Sculptra, are advanced injectable treatments designed to stimulate your body’s natural collagen production for gradual, long-lasting rejuvenation. Rather than adding immediate volume, these treatments work beneath the surface to improve skin quality, restore structure, and enhance overall facial balance over time. Common treatment areas may include the cheeks, temples, jawline, and areas of volume loss, helping to soften lines and create a more youthful, refreshed appearance. Results develop subtly and progressively, delivering natural-looking outcomes that continue to improve over several months.

Regenerative Injection (PRP/PRF)

45 minutes

PRP (platelet-rich plasma) and PRF (platelet-rich fibrin) injections are regenerative treatments that use your body’s own growth factors to stimulate healing, collagen production, and tissue renewal. A small sample of your blood is processed and strategically injected into targeted areas to improve skin quality, restore volume, and support natural rejuvenation. These treatments are commonly used for under-eye rejuvenation, facial enhancement, and hair restoration, helping to improve texture, tone, and overall vitality. PRF, a more advanced form, contains a higher concentration of growth factors and fibrin for a slower, sustained release—enhancing results over time.

Kybella

30 minutes

Kybella® injections are a non-surgical treatment designed to permanently reduce unwanted fat in areas such as the under chin, jowls, bra fat, and other small pockets of stubborn fat. This injectable contains deoxycholic acid, which works to break down and eliminate fat cells—resulting in a more defined, contoured appearance over time. Treatments are quick and minimally invasive, with a series of sessions recommended based on your individual anatomy and goals. As fat cells are destroyed, results are long-lasting and continue to improve with each session.

Microneedling

60 minutes

Microneedling is an advanced skin rejuvenation treatment designed to stimulate collagen production and improve overall skin quality. Using an FDA-cleared device like SkinPen, controlled micro-injuries are created in the skin to activate the body’s natural healing response—resulting in smoother texture, improved tone, and a more radiant, refined complexion. This treatment is ideal for addressing fine lines, acne scars, enlarged pores, and uneven skin tone. Over time, microneedling helps strengthen the skin from within, creating firmer, healthier-looking skin.

Chemical Peels

30 minutes

Chemical peels are advanced skin resurfacing treatments designed to improve tone, texture, and overall skin clarity. Using carefully selected medical-grade solutions, the outer layers of the skin are gently exfoliated to promote cellular turnover and reveal a smoother, brighter, more even complexion. These treatments are ideal for addressing concerns such as acne, hyperpigmentation, fine lines, sun damage, and dull skin. Peels can be customized in strength and formulation to suit your skin type and desired level of correction—from light refreshers to more corrective treatments.

Revision DEJ BioStim Treatment

30 minutes

Revision Skincare DEJ BioStim Treatment is an advanced regenerative treatment designed to stimulate collagen and elastin production at a deeper level, helping to strengthen the skin’s foundation and improve overall quality. Ideal for concerns such as thinning skin, fine lines, loss of elasticity, and early signs of aging, it works to restore firmness, smooth texture, and support long-term skin health. Often paired with microneedling or other advanced treatments, it enhances penetration and optimizes results for a more refined, rejuvenated appearance.

Express Dermaplane

30 minutes

Dermaplaning is a gentle, non-invasive exfoliation treatment designed to instantly smooth and brighten the skin. Using a precision blade, the outer layer of dead skin cells and fine vellus hair (“peach fuzz”) is carefully removed, revealing a softer, more radiant complexion. This treatment enhances product absorption and creates a flawless canvas for makeup application while improving overall skin texture and tone. Dermaplaning is ideal for those looking for immediate results with no downtime.
